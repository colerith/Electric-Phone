# Electric Phone avatar library v1

312 images generated locally with DiceBear 9.4.2 (240 world avatars, 72 anonymous avatars). These are not Pinterest downloads or official Discord defaults. Styles are remixes by DiceBear of the artists' original works. Changes: seeded combinations, pastel background, rasterized/resized to 240px WebP. Individual seeds and hashes are in manifest.json.

The artwork retains its original CC0 / CC BY 4.0 terms; Electric Phone's usage restrictions do not apply to these assets. No artist endorsement is implied.
